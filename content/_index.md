Hi, my name is Francesco Verrengia, but you can call me Verryx.
I think my [README](https://github.com/Verryx-02) already describes me pretty well, but there are a few things worth digging into here.

I'm writing this blog to document my projects and what I learn along the way, from the homelab to software, security and AI.

<!--more-->

I do it for a few reasons:

* To share my work with the community of people in my situation, so they can take inspiration from it, or roast me if I've done something very wrong without noticing.
* To remember everything I did and why I did it that way.
* To speed up disaster recovery, should it ever be needed.
* To show the companies I'd like to work for my point of view, my experience, and the mistakes I've made on my own infrastructure, so that I don't repeat them on theirs.

I figured that documenting all the effort I put in, and explaining the reasoning behind my choices, is worth far more than a post on that strange corner of the internet full of pointless motivational posts called LinkedIn.

I write these posts myself. AI only helps me proofread them and translate them into English, but the stories, the ideas and the mistakes are all mine. Not because I hate AI, quite the opposite: I'm a big enthusiast, I study it, use it constantly and keep up with every new development. But I want these posts to be mine and to reflect who I am.

This is my story, and nobody can tell it better than me.
